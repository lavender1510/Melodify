## Scope

- 114 color variants from 14 families are available in Melodify Settings.
- The original eight Melodify themes and user-created themes remain unchanged.
- Turntable from the archive contains no defined colors and is not treated as
  a color scheme.
- This is a **palette adaptation**, not a pixel-for-pixel port of the original
  Spicetify CSS layouts, fonts, images, scripts, or animations.
- No third-party fonts, images, or executable theme scripts are included.
